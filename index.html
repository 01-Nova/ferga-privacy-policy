Ferga Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Ferga respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Ferga application.

2. Information Collection

Ferga is designed to operate locally on your computer. The application does not intentionally collect or transmit your personal information to the developer.

3. Local Data Storage

Information created or entered in Ferga is stored locally on your computer, where applicable. The developer does not receive this locally stored information.

You are responsible for protecting your computer and keeping backups of important data.

4. Internet and Third-Party Services

Ferga does not intentionally send user information to external servers or third-party services as part of its normal operation. If this changes in a future version, this policy will be updated.

5. Data Sharing

The developer does not intentionally collect or share personal information through Ferga.

6. Security

Users should protect their computers and maintain backups. No electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if Ferga's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this same URL.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, use the support contact or support webpage provided in the Ferga Microsoft Store listing.
